Your learning is personal.

Updated 12 September 2026

WordMemo helps you remember Bible verses, alone or with a private life group. There are no ads or app analytics.

What is saved

Guest practice is stored in your browser or device. When you sign in, your email and basic account profile are handled by Amazon Cognito. If you choose Google, Google provides your email and basic profile through its sign-in service; WordMemo does not receive your Google password or request access to your contacts, files, or messages.

Your signed-in reviews, learning schedule, settings, favourites, rewards, and private verse stacks are saved in an encrypted AWS DynamoDB database in Oregon, USA. A cached copy stays on the device for offline practice. Sign-in tokens are kept for the browser tab's session; the sign-in service also uses authentication cookies and temporary redirect storage.

What your group sees

Your group sees the display name you choose and stacks you share. Progress sharing starts off. If you enable it for a group, members see your points, level, practice-day count, and number of verses remembered. They do not receive your email or detailed review history.

A group invitation lets anyone holding that link join after signing in. Invitations expire after seven days or when the owner replaces the link. Shared stacks are copies: another member can keep a copy after the original is removed. Practice rooms show collective response counts and your own response, with no names beside incorrect answers.

Retention and control

Active account progress has no automatic expiry. Superseded progress snapshots are marked for deletion after seven days. AWS point-in-time recovery can retain database backups for up to 35 days. Rooms expire after six hours; temporary responses are marked for deletion afterwards, which AWS performs asynchronously. Closing a group revokes access; its stored contents are retained for recovery.

You can turn progress sharing off, leave a group, export a backup, and sign out in the app. Cached account copies remain on your device after signing out, so use your own browser profile on shared devices. Clearing browser data removes those local copies. To request account and database deletion, contact the person who provided WordMemo to your group; the release includes an operator deletion procedure. Copies you deliberately shared and others saved may remain with those people.

Service operation

AWS processes hosting, sign-in, API, database, and basic operational information needed to provide the service. The app's API does not log passwords, access tokens, invitation tokens, email addresses, or review bodies. Error logs contain a request identifier and expire after 14 days. Publisher links open eBible.org under its own practices.

Questions? Contact your WordMemo app owner through the person who shared this app with you.
